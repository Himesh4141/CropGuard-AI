Critical training risk

The augmented classes are built from only 4 bacterial, 19 fungal, 16 pests, and 15 healthy indexed source groups. The 2,274 variants are not 2,274 independent specimens. Random splitting of these variants would leak image-family features and inflate evaluation metrics. Do not deploy a four-class disease predictor based on these labels.

What pictures can and cannot show

Examples show visible wilting, necrosis, spots, curling and pest-like damage, but appearance alone does not prove a bacterial or fungal pathogen. Some images have broad-leaved basil-like plants, and species identity must be checked. Images marked “pests” include snail/insect examples that may not be Holy Basil. Disease names such as leaf spot, mildew, blight, rot and nutrient stress are candidate hypotheses, not confirmed diagnoses from this archive.

Next decisive research step: get confirmed plant species, pathogen-specific labels from a qualified reviewer, licensing for the Kaggle originals, more independent real-phone photos, and a farm/plant-level held-out test set. Money Plant requires its own dataset. For the Home Planters app, an honest local photo + observation workflow can operate now without inventing an AI prediction.
